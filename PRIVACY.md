# Privacy Policy – Vinyl Player for YouTube Music
# 隱私權政策 – Vinyl Player for YouTube Music

_Last updated / 最後更新: 2026-11-20_

## English

**Vinyl Player for YouTube Music** ("the extension") is an unofficial extension and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by YouTube or Google.

### Data we collect
**We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data on our own servers.** The extension has no backend server and no analytics or tracking.

### Data stored on your device
- **Settings and your Gemini API key** (optional) are stored locally in your browser, on the music.youtube.com site storage. They are never sent to the developer.
- **Translation cache** (translated lyrics) is stored locally in your browser to avoid repeated requests.

You can remove this data at any time by clearing site data for music.youtube.com or uninstalling the extension.

### Third-party services
The extension sends requests directly from your browser to:

| Service | What is sent | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| lrclib.net | Song title, artist, album and duration of the current track | Fetch synced lyrics |
| Google Gemini API (generativelanguage.googleapis.com) | Lyrics text and your own API key | Optional AI lyrics translation (only when you enable it and provide a key) |

These services have their own privacy policies:
- lrclib.net: https://lrclib.net
- Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy

### Permissions
- `https://music.youtube.com/*`: to restyle the page and display the vinyl player and lyrics.
- Host access to `lrclib.net` and Google API domains: only for the purposes listed above.

### Children
The extension is not directed at children and collects no personal information.

### Changes
Updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date.

## 繁體中文

**Vinyl Player for YouTube Music**(以下簡稱「本擴充功能」)為非官方擴充功能,與 YouTube 或 Google 無任何關聯,亦未獲其認可或贊助。

### 我們收集的資料
**我們不會在自己的伺服器上收集、儲存、出售或分享任何個人資料。** 本擴充功能沒有後端伺服器,也沒有任何分析或追蹤工具。

### 儲存在你裝置上的資料
- **設定與你的 Gemini API Key**(選填)只儲存在你瀏覽器中 music.youtube.com 的網站儲存空間,不會傳送給開發者。
- **翻譯快取**(已翻譯的歌詞)儲存在你的瀏覽器,用來避免重複請求。

你可以隨時清除 music.youtube.com 的網站資料,或解除安裝擴充功能來移除這些資料。

### 第三方服務
本擴充功能會直接從你的瀏覽器向下列服務發送請求:

| 服務 | 傳送內容 | 用途 |
|---|---|---|
| lrclib.net | 目前播放歌曲的歌名、歌手、專輯與長度 | 取得同步歌詞 |
| Google Gemini API(generativelanguage.googleapis.com) | 歌詞文字與你自己的 API Key | 選用的 AI 歌詞翻譯(僅在你啟用並提供 Key 時) |

這些服務有各自的隱私權政策:
- lrclib.net:https://lrclib.net
- Google:https://policies.google.com/privacy

### 權限說明
- `https://music.youtube.com/*`:用於調整頁面外觀,並顯示黑膠播放器與歌詞。
- `lrclib.net` 與 Google API 網域的存取權:僅用於上述用途。

### 兒童
本擴充功能並非針對兒童,也不會收集個人資訊。

政策若有更新,會在此頁面公告並更新日期。
